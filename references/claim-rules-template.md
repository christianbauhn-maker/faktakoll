## Data quality standards

Define the minimum requirements for figures you will publish.

**Required for every numerical claim:**
- [ ] Named primary source
- [ ] Year of publication
- [ ] Confidence tier assigned (High / Medium / Composite estimate)

**Composite estimates** (figures derived from multiple surveys):
- Must be labeled explicitly in the text
- May not serve as the central claim in an argument
- Must list the component sources

**Projections and forecasts:**
- Must be labeled as projections
- Must name the organization that produced the forecast
- Must include the year the forecast was published
